# tasacion.com audit report

Date: 2026-09-13. Codex CLI gpt-6-astra (effort low, session 01a09813-4d01-7cd1-a541-0884435f145d) scanned the source repo at C:\Claude 1\tasacion (cloned from antonmarklundcom/tasacion). Fable 5.1 ran all live HTTP checks with curl and verified every top-10 item. Labels: [Codex] found by gpt-6-astra, [Fable] found or verified by the manager.

## 1. Verdict

tasacion.com is not your site: it serves a "this domain may be for sale" parking page from abovedomains.com, and every path on it returns that page with HTTP 200 [Fable]. The real site is tasacion.com.py, and it is technically solid: all 15 sitemap URLs return 200, robots and sitemap are correct, every page has unique meta, one H1, canonical, viewport, alt text, OG image and valid JSON-LD, gzip is on, and unknown paths return a real 404 [Codex, Fable]. The weak spots are the lead path and the copy: leads can be lost silently, there is no privacy page, the seller service is described as both official and not official, and analytics is off.

## 2. Top 10 issues by impact

1. **Domain mismatch, high** [Fable]. tasacion.com and www.tasacion.com show a parking page; DNS points to 103.224.182.246. All canonicals and sitemap URLs use .com.py [Codex]. Fix: buy the .com and 301 it to .com.py, or stop calling the site tasacion.com anywhere.
2. **Contact form false success, high** [Codex, verified by Fable]. lead-forward.php writes the lead with @file_put_contents, ignores the result, and always redirects to /gracias.html even if logging and CRM forwarding both fail. Fix: check both results and show an error when nothing accepted the lead.
3. **Phone error lands on a dead anchor, high** [Codex, verified by Fable]. lead-forward.php line 61 rejects phones under 6 characters and redirects to /?error=telefono#contacto. The home page has no id="contacto" and nothing reads the error, so the user sees nothing. Fix: redirect back to /contacto/ with a visible error, and add minlength on the input.
4. **No privacy or legal page, high** [Codex, verified by Fable]. /privacidad/ is 404, the footer has no privacy or RUC line, and the form collects name, phone and email for a CRM. Fix: add /privacidad/ and link it in the footer and beside the form.
5. **Seller service contradiction, high** [Codex, verified by Fable]. /informes-periciales/ says "Mismo informe; si firmás exclusividad ... el costo se descuenta" while the home page says the seller appraisal "No es un informe oficial ni tiene validez legal ni bancaria". Fix: pick one definition in content.mjs and align every card and CTA.
6. **Mortgage JSON-LD price wrong, medium** [Codex, verified by Fable]. /tasaciones/hipotecaria/ Offer has minPrice 800000 and maxPrice 1500000, but the visible price is "Gs. 1.500.000 + IVA" upward. Fix: read the credit band from PRECIOS.credito in serviceJsonLd().
7. **Broken hero link, medium** [Codex, verified by Fable]. "Ver qué incluye" on /informes-periciales/ points to #incluye; no such id exists. Fix: point it to #compraventa or add the id.
8. **No conversion tracking, medium** [Codex]. ANALYTICS_ID is empty so the loader never runs; WhatsApp clicks only reach dataLayer. Fix: set a GA4 or GTM id in build-site.mjs.
9. **Seller cost claim under-qualified, medium** [Codex, verified by Fable]. Hero and footer say "costo cubierto por tu corredor" without the exclusivity condition. Fix: add "con exclusividad" next to those CTAs.
10. **Pricing copy contradiction, medium** [Codex]. /informes-periciales/ says price does not depend on size, then says the range depends on type and size. Fix: purpose sets the band, type and size set the quote within it.

## 3. Everything else by category

- **Links and mixed content**: all internal links return 200, no http:// assets [Codex, Fable].
- **Meta**: hipotecaria and valuacion-para-vender descriptions are too long. Sitemap is hand-maintained [Codex].
- **Schema**: areaServed is hard-coded to Asunción even for campos, which claims nationwide coverage [Codex].
- **Performance**: tasacion-de-inmuebles-asuncion-1920.webp is 360 KB, the only asset over 300 KB. site.js has no defer. Fonts come from Google, not self-hosted [Codex, Fable].
- **Copy**: FAQ succession answer lacks an article ("lo firma Perito Tasador"); a pricing paragraph starts lowercase "desde Gs."; footer year is empty until JS runs [Codex, Fable].
- **Content**: /contacto/ has 222 words; other pages 412 to 1165 [Codex].
- **Dead code**: unused exports PRECIO and CRED_SELLO_CORTA, orphan CSS rules, legacy zonas wiring in build-images.mjs, six unreferenced images (310 KB) in the deploy zip [Codex].
- **Positives**: WhatsApp CTA above the fold on every page; hero is clear [Codex, Fable].
